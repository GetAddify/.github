# Addify

**Commercial Auto Insurance for the Modern Fleet Economy**

Addify is a technology-powered platform that provides specialized commercial auto insurance solutions for vehicle rental businesses and car-sharing hosts. We partner with industry-rated insurance carriers to offer affordable, flexible coverage designed specifically for the rental and fleet market.

## What We Offer

- **Commercial Auto Programs** - Save up to 40% compared to traditional providers
- **Flexible Coverage Options** - Including liability, physical damage, and roadside assistance
- **Off-Rent Program (ORP)** - Coverage when your vehicle isn't being rented
- **Continuous Coverage Program (CCP)** - Secondary protection for rental agencies and fleets
- **Streamlined Claims Management** - We handle the claims process for you
- **Easy Account Management** - Add/remove vehicles and manage policies through our platform

## Who We Serve

- Private car rental operators
- Car-sharing hosts (Getaround, Turo, etc.)
- Independent rental agencies
- Fleet managers

Currently available in Florida, Georgia, Maryland, Arizona, Alabama, and Tennessee, with more states coming soon.
